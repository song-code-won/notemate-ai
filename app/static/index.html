⚙️ AI & System Settings

Configure local open-weight model integration, verify Ollama connection, and review privacy guarantees.

Local Model Configuration

NoteMate AI connects to Ollama running on your machine for zero-cost, private inference.

Default local port is 11434.
Recommended: gemma3:1b or gemma3:4b for fast consumer laptop inference.

🌱 Why Open Innovation Matters

  • 🔒 100% Privacy & Data Ownership: Your personal notes, thoughts, and audio recordings never leave your machine. No cloud telemetry or third-party training.
  • 💸 Zero Subscription Cost: Runs completely free without paid API tokens (OpenAI, Claude, etc.).
  • 🔄 Model Flexibility: Easily switch models (Gemma 3, Llama 3, Mistral, Qwen) by changing the model tag.
  • 🔍 Full Transparency: All code, prompts, and database schemas are open-source and auditable.

Quick Setup Guide for Ollama

  1. Download Ollama from ollama.com.
  2. Open your terminal and pull Gemma 3: ollama pull gemma3:1b
  3. Ollama runs in the background at http://localhost:11434 automatically.
